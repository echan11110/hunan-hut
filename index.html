Our Story

A Neighborhood
Institution

For over 25 years, Hunan Hut has been a cornerstone of the Adelphi and Langley Park community. What started as a small carry-out has grown into something far greater — a place where neighbors become regulars, and regulars become family.

Our kitchen runs on dedication: fresh ingredients prepared daily, recipes honed over decades, and a team that genuinely cares about every order that goes out the window. The staff speaks English and Spanish, because this community is ours too.

Customers who came as toddlers now bring their own children. People drive 30 minutes out of their way because nothing else compares. That loyalty is everything to us — and we honor it with every plate.
