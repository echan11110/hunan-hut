What is mambo sauce?

Mambo sauce is a sweet, tangy red sauce that's a Washington, DC–area tradition, especially at Chinese carry-outs. It's best known on fried chicken wings, and our Wings with Mambo Sauce are our best seller.

What are your most popular dishes?

Our best seller is Wings with Mambo Sauce. Other favorites include Triple Delight, Buffalo Wings with French Fries, Shrimp Lo Mein, Chicken & Broccoli, and Singapore Rice Noodle.

Does Hunan Hut have chicken wings?

Yes, wings are our best seller. Order fried wings on their own ($2.95 for one, up to $17.95 for eight) or as a Chicken Special with steamed rice, fried rice, or French fries (from $6.50). Choose mambo sauce, ketchup, chili oil, or hot sauce; extra sauce is 25¢. We also make Buffalo Wings in hot buffalo sauce, in orders of 8, 12, or 16.

What noodle dishes does Hunan Hut have?

Lo mein (vegetable, chicken, pork, beef, shrimp, combination, or seafood); rice noodles made fresh in-house, including our spicy Singapore Rice Noodle with roast pork, chicken, and shrimp in curry sauce; and chow fun or drunken noodle made with fresh flat noodles. Shrimp Lo Mein is one of our customer favorites.
