Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Hunan Hut, the family-owned Chinese carry-out at 7967 New Hampshire Ave in Adelphi, MD.

What are Hunan Hut's hours?

We're open seven days a week: Sunday–Thursday 11 AM–10 PM and Friday–Saturday 11 AM–11 PM. Hours can change, so call (301) 439-3886 to confirm.

Where is Hunan Hut located?

7967 New Hampshire Ave, Adelphi, MD 20783, minutes from Hyattsville and Langley Park and a short drive from the University of Maryland in College Park.

Does Hunan Hut deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash. DoorDash orders are taken until 9:25 PM Sunday–Thursday and until 10:25 PM Friday–Saturday. Prices on DoorDash are higher than our menu to cover DoorDash's fees; for our regular prices, call ahead and pick up.

How do I order for pickup?

Call (301) 439-3886 and your order will be ready when you arrive, or walk in and order at the counter.

What is mambo sauce?

Mambo sauce is a sweet, tangy red sauce that's a Washington, DC–area tradition, especially at Chinese carry-outs. It's best known on fried chicken wings, and our Wings with Mambo Sauce are our best seller.

What are your most popular dishes?

Our best seller is Wings with Mambo Sauce. Other favorites include Triple Delight, Buffalo Wings with French Fries, Shrimp Lo Mein, Chicken & Broccoli, and Singapore Rice Noodle.

Do you have spicy dishes?

Yes. Spicy dishes are marked with 🌶️ on our menu, including General Tso's Chicken, Triple Delight, Singapore Rice Noodle, and Buffalo Wings.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Our menu has a vegetable section and tofu dishes, including General Tso's Tofu.

Do menu prices include tax?

Yes, all menu prices include tax. Prices can change, so check the menu or call to confirm.

Is Hunan Hut family-owned?

Yes. Hunan Hut has been family-owned and serving Adelphi and the surrounding neighborhoods since 1999, more than 25 years.
